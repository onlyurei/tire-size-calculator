What you can compare

Tire sizes and formats

Compare passenger and light-truck metric sizes from 125 to 405 mm wide with profiles from 20 to 85. The calculator also handles legacy full-profile sizes, applicable 82-series equivalents, and flotation sizes entered by diameter, width, and rim size. Motorcycle and ATV/UTV formats include metric, inch, and alphabetic designations where supported, with rim-code and construction distinctions.

Wheel weight and acceleration

For cars and light trucks, enter the tire and wheel weights, vehicle weight, peak torque, and 0–60 time. TireCalc estimates the change in total mass and rotational inertia across all four wheels, then gives a range of equivalent vehicle weights during acceleration based on how that mass might be distributed.

Measurements and speed

Compare diameter, circumference, section width, sidewall height, load index, speed rating, and actual speed when the speedometer reads 60 or 100. Switch between imperial and metric units, and use scaled full-circle or split-circle diagrams to see how the tires differ.

Replacement size options

Set how much rim diameter, section width, and circumference can change. Then pick a suggested car or light-truck size and compare it with your current setup. The built-in Size Guide explains supported formats and their limits.

Check fitment before buying or mounting. A recognized size does not establish vehicle compatibility. Verify load, speed, rim, clearance, and manufacturer requirements with the vehicle and tire manufacturers and a qualified tire professional. Do not infer an inch measurement from a motorcycle rim code.
